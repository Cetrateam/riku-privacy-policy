# Privacy Policy for Riku Puzzle Games

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Cetra Development ("we", "our") handles information when you use this mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Advertising Data

The free version of the game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information to provide personalized advertising, including:

* **Device Identifiers:** Such as the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) on iOS
* **Usage Data:** Information about ads viewed and interacted with

This data is collected by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. We do not have direct access to this data.

Ads are shown only in the free version. After purchasing the PRO version, no ads are requested or displayed.

### Purchase History

The game offers a one-time in-app purchase (PRO version). Purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information. Purchase history is used only on-device to verify your entitlements.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

Apart from the advertising data collected by Google AdMob described above, we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as:

* Name or contact information
* Location data
* Photos, videos, or other media
* Health or fitness data
* Financial information
* User-generated content

All your game data and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to any server.

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google's use of advertising identifiers and data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google is required to protect the data it collects with a level of protection equal to that described in this Privacy Policy and required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

You can control how your data is used for advertising:

* **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** On first launch, a consent form provided by Google is shown. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy settings inside the game.
* **Tracking on iOS:** Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track". If you do not allow tracking, the ads you see are not personalized.

### Game Center

If the game offers leaderboards, they are provided by Apple's Game Center. Using Game Center is optional and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data retention by us. Any data collected by AdMob is subject to Google's data retention policies.

You can delete all game data stored on your device at any time by deleting the app.

## Children's Privacy

Our games are not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertisements shown are provided by AdMob and are subject to Google's policies regarding ad content.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store support link for this game.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your data, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal information and to withdraw your consent. Since we do not collect personal data directly, you can change your advertising consent as described above, and requests about data collected through AdMob should be directed to Google.
